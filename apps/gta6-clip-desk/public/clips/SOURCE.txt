Synthetic placeholder clips
==========================

Files:
- harbor-cruise.mp4
- rooftop-hop.mp4
- lane-chase.mp4
- grid-pass.mp4

How they were made:
scripts/render-placeholders.sh builds each file with ffmpeg from lavfi color
sources and the overlay filter. Shapes move with per-frame expressions.
There is no recorded footage, no audio, and no third-party video.
